=============================================================================== SLIDE 2 — MODELLING · STRATEGIES
01 — ARCHITECTURE STRATEGY Multi-task core. One shared trunk -> 7 property heads. 5 of 7 targets (egb 337, eps 229, nc 229, ei 222, eea 221 rows) carry 5/7 of the score, so the abundant targets (tg 5,785 / egc 2,832) subsidise the scarce ones. Read the graph, not a hash of it. The MPNN takes atoms as nodes and bonds as edges — 4 message-passing rounds, GRU update, sum/mean/max readout. It is the ONLY member not built on the shared 5,443-column matrix, so its errors are structurally decorrelated. Best single model at 0.9077 mean OOF. Periodic graph. We drop the two * dummies, bond their neighbours and flag the wrap-around edge. Applied to 9,475 / 9,487 graphs. +0.0017 / +0.0108 over the monomer graph on two fair seed pairs.

02 — PHYSICS + DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE Physics applied AFTER stacking, not as a feature. A tree splits one axis at a time and cannot represent a sum of two columns, so ei = egc + eea is wasted as 1 feature among 5,443. As an explicit blend it reaches ~0.96 on covered rows where the stack reaches ~0.89. Ionic decomposition — the turning point. Maxwell's n² = eps holds at OPTICAL frequency, but the dataset tabulates the STATIC constant, so the raw relation scores only 0.336. DFPT captures exactly eps_elec + eps_ionic and nothing else (Chen, Kim, Batra et al., 2020), and those two parts are stored as separate fields in the dataset of record (Huan et al., Sci. Data 2016). Since eps_elec IS n², the shortfall is exactly the ionic term: eps − nc² = eps_ionic (an identity, not a regression) Non-negative by construction — confirmed on 134/134 co-observed molecules. RESULT: eps on covered rows 0.9295 -> 0.9605; nc 0.9653 -> 0.9772.

03 — DATA STRATEGY Canonicalisation at ingest — 67.6% of supplied SMILES are non-canonical; 10,605 raw strings are only 8,990 distinct molecules. Chain-extension augmentation — A and AA are the same polymer with the same labels, so the dimer is free data. Applied to the 5 scarce targets only: +0.0056 / +0.0085 on two independent fold seeds. Independently corroborated as a top-solution strategy (Liu et al., NeurIPS OPC report, arXiv:2512.08896). Size-adaptive capacity — one parameter set sized for tg over-fits a 220-row target: +0.0050 mean R², concentrated where needed (ei +0.018, nc +0.009, eps +0.007), tg/egc unchanged.

=============================================================================== SLIDE 4 — KEY RESULTS: THE THREE ROUND-3 THEMES *** OUR DIFFERENTIATOR ***
01 — POLYMER INVARIANCE: an exact guarantee, not a statistical one Canonicalising at ingest makes every downstream stage — descriptors, fingerprints, tokens, partner lookup — a deterministic function of the MOLECULE, and canonical(rewrite(s)) == canonical(s) for any valid rewriting. Certified at three levels, all PASS: L1 string 2,000 rewritings x 400 molecules 0 mismatches L2 features 5,581 cols x 150 molecules max |Δ| = 0.000e+00 L3 predictions 1,500 model-molecule predictions max |Δ| = 0.000e+00 Diagnostic that guided the design: Morgan fingerprints and RDKit descriptors were ALREADY invariant (0/400 changed under rewriting). The character-level CNN was the ONLY non-invariant component (spread 43% of its across-molecule spread). Canonicalisation closes it to exactly 0. The MPNN is invariant by construction — a graph carries no atom ordering.

02 — EXPLAINABILITY: exact TreeSHAP, and it recovers known physics Computed inside LightGBM via pred_contrib=True — the same algorithm the shap package calls, with no external dependency that can fail at runtime. ELECTRONIC (egc/egb/ei/eea) — saturation vs conjugation is the master variable. egb: po_conj_ratio 7.25%, rd_FractionCSP3 6.77%, po_fsp3 5.57%. An sp³-rich backbone localises electrons in sigma bonds -> WIDE gap; extended conjugation delocalises pi density -> NARROW gap. Sulfur is the second lever (rd_fr_C_S is the single largest eea attribution at 6.14%). OPTICAL (nc/eps) — the SAME axis with the OPPOSITE sign. po_conj_ratio 6.91% and rd_FractionCSP3 6.52% top nc, and molar-refractivity surface-area descriptors (rd_SMR_VSA5/10) appear for both. The model RECOVERS the Lorentz–Lorenz relation from structure without being given it — consistent with n treated as electronic polarisability (Lightstone, Batra et al., 2020). THERMAL (tg) — po_rot_ratio alone carries 16.70%, po_ring_ratio 6.42%: chain flexibility dominates, exactly as free-volume theory predicts. DESIGN GUIDANCE for a new polymer: to widen the gap, break conjugation with sp³ spacers and avoid thioether linkages; to raise refractive index, use polarisable but NON-conjugated heavy atoms — sulfur without extended pi.

03 — ROBUSTNESS: a calibrated applicability domain Novelty from UNFOLDED Morgan substructure hashes — folded 2048-bit fingerprints are degenerate here (every bit is occupied by a 5.97 M corpus, so novelty collapses to a constant). A molecule is out-of-domain when it contains a substructure absent from the entire corpus. That flag predicts error: target n in R² in n out R² out |err| in |err| out tg 3026 0.9351 2759 0.9267 16.55 20.50 egc 1486 0.9474 1346 0.9215 0.211 0.334 eps 73 0.9182 156 0.8532 0.169 0.285 50.9% of test rows are flagged and ship with this caveat. HONEST NEGATIVE RESULT: we tested the corpus three ways as MODEL INPUT and none helped — TF-IDF+SVD embedding +0.0012 (noise), novelty features ~0, domain-aware recalibration −0.0049. The SVD basis is a linear re-expression of the Morgan fingerprint the trees already see in full. We repurposed the corpus rather than dropping it.

=============================================================================== SLIDE 5 — DETAILS OF RELEVANT EXPERIMENTATION AND METHODOLOGY
01 — ABLATION STUDIES AND IMPROVEMENTS Leave-one-out on saved OOF vectors is how we found dead models. Rather than guessing, we removed each model from the stack and re-measured across 3 fold seeds. Dropping CatBoost IMPROVED the stack (+0.0003 — it was actively harmful); the CNN was exactly +0.0000. The NN (−0.0061, and −0.0217 on ei alone) and the MPNN (−0.0048) carried everything. nn + mpnn + lgbm alone scored 0.9176, matching all six models. What that bought. CatBoost + CNN together cost 2 h 08 m of a 5 h 32 m run (CNN 1 h 36 m, CatBoost 32 m). Removing them cost +0.0006 in score and freed that time, which we REINVESTED as more seeds: NN 5 -> 8, MPNN 3 -> 5. Attribution, honestly. The 0.916 -> 0.922 gain bundles four changes. Our measured/estimated split: model-set pruning +0.0006, shrink 1.0 +0.0002, extra seeds ~+0.002, more training data ~+0.003. The seeds helped; the extra data helped more. We do NOT claim dropping the CNN raised the score — it bought compute at no cost, and the compute raised the score. Measured and REJECTED (recorded so they are not retried): KernelRidge 0.7627 and multi-task LightGBM 0.8266 — both sit on the same feature matrix, so the stack pays them nothing. Trimers were worse than dimers. Physics as STACK FEATURES scored −0.0007, worse than the post-hoc blend (which calibrates on covered rows only). Leaderboard noise, simulated. Two identical models differ by >= 0.007 half the time; the entire visible 0.889–0.920 spread fits inside one confidence band. This reframed the goal from chasing positions to chasing signal.

Why we submitted the slower one. The competition scores accuracy, not throughput. Both fit inside the 12 h Kaggle limit with margin, and inference cost is identical either way — 14.2 ms/sample — because the extra time is TRAINING, paid once. We optimised the metric that is actually scored.

But the honest reading is more interesting than "we picked the higher number." ppp-v4-g reaches within 0.003 of our submission in LESS THAN HALF the runtime, using 45% fewer features and WITHOUT feeding archive rows into training. Two things follow: • Its polymer-grouped folds are the more defensible design. A plain KFold can place the same molecule in train and validation across different property rows; GroupKFold cannot. Its own notes record the better CV-to-LB transfer (−0.020 vs −0.041) that this buys. • 0.003 is INSIDE our measured leaderboard noise band of +/-0.007. We are therefore honest that these two pipelines are statistically indistinguishable — we submitted the higher score, but we do not claim it is a better model.

What we would carry forward: ppp-v4-g's grouped folds on round3_v2's larger feature set and training data. That combination was identified but not run — it is the first thing we would do with more compute budget.

04 — METHODOLOGY / LLM-ASSISTED WORKFLOW LLM used as an EXPERIMENTAL INSTRUMENT, not a code generator. The loop was: form a hypothesis -> measure it offline on saved OOF vectors (seconds, not a 6 h rerun) -> keep only what beat the FULL stack, never a weaker baseline. Disciplines that mattered most: • oof_and_preds.npz persists every base-model OOF and test vector, so a blending experiment costs seconds instead of a rerun • Three early estimates were WRONG because they benchmarked against a LightGBM-only baseline instead of the full stack+blend — we now always compare against the deployed pipeline • An end-to-end harness (28 assertions) runs the real notebook on real data before every Kaggle submission • Section 5 PROVES the leak exists first, then proves each guard removes it — a guard that passes without a demonstrable leak proves nothing

KNOWN LIMITATION, STATED BEFORE WE ARE ASKED "SMILES invariance" is three separate claims and only one is exact: • Re-representation (atom ordering) — EXACT, Δ = 0, certified • Translational (same chain, different cut point) — NOT guaranteed. Measured on 985 true cut-point translations: canonical SMILES agree 7.9%, Morgan 10.1%, periodic graph 16.4% (45.8% ignoring the wrap-bond flag, 88.7% on topology alone). Diagnosis: our wrap-around edge is forced to a generic single bond and carries a positional flag — both cut-point dependent by definition. The fix is contained and identified. • Repetition (A vs AA) — APPROXIMATE, trained in via chain extension.
